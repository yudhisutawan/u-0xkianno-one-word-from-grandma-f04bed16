# Candidates — One Word From Grandma

Judge criteria (in order, from competition.yaml / RULES.txt):
1. Reads as the single word a grandma would text about AI.
2. Sounds like a real grandmother's texting voice — warm, a little baffled.
3. Funny in one word, joke lands without caption or context.
4. Tied to AI, robots or machines (not a word that fits any subject).
5. Fresh pick, not the obvious one, instantly readable.

| # | Word | Why it was considered | Weakness |
|---|------|----------------------|----------|
| A | Alexa | The AI in grandma's house; she talks to it like a person | Most obvious pick in the field; likely house anchor |
| B | Roomba | Her robot vacuum, talked about like a pet | Slightly less "AI" than a voice assistant |
| C | Contraption | Classic grandma word for a machine she doesn't understand | Generic machine word, weak AI tie |
| D | Doohickey | Peak grandma vocabulary | Fits any subject; weak AI tie |
| E | Gizmo | Grandma word for gadgets | Generic, mild joke |
| F | Al | Grandma misreading "AI" as a man named Al | Joke needs the visual pun; judge may read it as just a name |
| G | Beepboop | Robot noises, funny | Robot voice, not grandma's voice |
| H | Tinman | Her generation's robot (Wizard of Oz) | Movie reference more than a texting word |
