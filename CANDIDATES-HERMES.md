# One Word From Grandma - AI Bounty Candidates

This document presents 8 distinct one-word candidate entries for the "One Word From Grandma" make.fun bounty competition, evaluated against the judge criteria:
1. Reads as the single word a grandma would text about AI
2. Sounds like a real grandmother texting voice, warm and a little baffled
3. Funny in one word with no context needed
4. Tied to AI, robots, or machines
5. Fresh, not the obvious pick, still instantly readable

---

## Candidates

### 1. Knowitall
- **Word:** Knowitall
- **Reasoning:** Captures a grandmother's affectionate yet exasperated reaction to an LLM that always has an immediate answer for everything without being asked.
- **Strongest Criterion:** #1 (Reads as the single word a grandma would text about AI)

### 2. Gremlin
- **Word:** Gremlin
- **Reasoning:** Evokes the classic grandparental explanation for when technology behaves unpredictably, hallucinates, or glitches out ("must be a gremlin in the machine").
- **Strongest Criterion:** #2 (Sounds like a real grandmother texting voice, warm and a little baffled)

### 3. Gentleman
- **Word:** Gentleman
- **Reasoning:** Perfectly targets a grandma being utterly charmed by ChatGPT's excessively polite, deferential conversational tone ("such a polite gentleman").
- **Strongest Criterion:** #5 (Fresh, not the obvious pick, still instantly readable)

### 4. Whippersnapper
- **Word:** Whippersnapper
- **Reasoning:** Applies an old-fashioned, affectionate scolding term to sassy modern chatbots and smart algorithms acting too smart for their britches.
- **Strongest Criterion:** #3 (Funny in one word with no context needed)

### 5. Busybody
- **Word:** Busybody
- **Reasoning:** Personifies AI assistants, autocomplete popups, and unsolicited chat boxes as an overly nosy neighbor living inside the television screen.
- **Strongest Criterion:** #4 (Tied to AI, robots, or machines)

### 6. Genie
- **Word:** Genie
- **Reasoning:** Expresses the whimsical, slightly bewildered wonder of typing a sentence and watching a machine instantly conjure up text, art, or answers.
- **Strongest Criterion:** #5 (Fresh, striking yet instantly readable)

### 7. Copycat
- **Word:** Copycat
- **Reasoning:** Nails the skeptical grandparental insight that AI isn't truly thinking, it's just expertly mimicking human writing and art.
- **Strongest Criterion:** #4 (Tied to AI mechanics)

### 8. Brainbox
- **Word:** Brainbox
- **Reasoning:** An endearingly quirky old-school term for a machine or device that seems to have a mind of its own.
- **Strongest Criterion:** #2 (Warm and a little baffled)

---

## Ranking of Candidates

1. **Knowitall** (Top Pick) - Immediacy, universal recognition, and pitch-perfect grandma voice for AI.
2. **Gremlin** - Hilarious, highly relatable tech-bafflement.
3. **Gentleman** - Subverts expectations with delightful warmth regarding AI politeness.
4. **Whippersnapper** - Great humor and generational contrast.
5. **Busybody** - Captures the intrusive nature of modern AI interfaces.
6. **Genie** - Captures the magical prompt experience.
7. **Copycat** - Sharp, grounded critique of LLMs in grandma terms.
8. **Brainbox** - Classic affectionate term, slightly less punchy than Knowitall.

## Top Pick
**Knowitall**
